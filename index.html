Mic ANC Lab

Experimental noise cancelling using your phone's microphone and any wired earphones.

Read first. Put the earphones in your ears before pressing Start, and keep the strength low. If you hear a howl, you have feedback: lower the strength or the phone volume.

This blocks sound you may need to hear. Do not use it while walking near traffic, cycling or driving. It works best on steady low sounds such as a fan, AC or engine hum, and does little for voices.

How it works

The phone mic hears the noise, a filter keeps only the low frequencies, and the app plays the opposite signal into your earphones. Phone audio has a delay of roughly 10 to 60 ms, so the predictive mode learns the noise pattern and plays what the noise will be a moment from now. This works for repeating sounds, not for sudden ones.

Needs HTTPS (for example GitHub Pages) or localhost for the browser to allow microphone access. Bluetooth earphones add 100 ms or more of delay, which makes this ineffective. Use wired earphones.
